A good corporate souvenir has one goal: **it gets used, so your logo is seen every day**. That means choosing items people actually need at their desk, in meetings, or on the go.

Here are ideas by type of event.

## Seminars, training, and workshops

Participants need something to carry materials and something to drink from. A common, useful kit:

- **Book/document bag** for folders, notebooks, and a thin laptop. Available in polyester, calico, or spunbond, with single-color or full-color DTF printing.
- **Tumbler** so participants do not rely on bottled water.
- **Bookmark** as an inexpensive but memorable extra.

## Gifts for clients and partners

For clients, a premium feel matters more than quantity. Choose one good item rather than several ordinary ones.

- **Logo-engraved tumbler.** The logo is permanently etched and looks elegant. Tumbler 001 (500ml) and the wood-pattern Tumbler 002 (500ml) both include engraving.
- **Bamboo crafts** such as a food carrier or basket bag as a hamper container. Ideal for companies that want to highlight local and eco-friendly values.

## Employee appreciation and company anniversaries

For large quantities on a limited budget, choose everyday items:

- **Logo mug.** The Lidded Custom Logo Mug (350ml) is one of the most affordable options and comes in many colors.
- **Bamboo pencil holder** for desks.
- **Bottle/tumbler bag** for employees who travel often.

## Tips for preparing a corporate souvenir order

1. **Set a budget per person.** This tells you whether to give one premium item or a kit with several items.
2. **Prepare a good logo file.** Use a vector format (AI, SVG, PDF) or a high-resolution PNG with a transparent background. A blurry logo produces a blurry print.
3. **Match the technique to the material.** Engraving for metal tumblers, screen printing or DTF for fabric bags. Read our [guide to engraving, screen printing, and full print](/en/blog/engraving-vs-printing/).
4. **Order well in advance.** Custom items need production time. Share your event date when ordering so the schedule can be confirmed.
5. **Add a buffer.** Order a few extra units for additional guests or replacements.
