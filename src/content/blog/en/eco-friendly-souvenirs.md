The least eco-friendly souvenir is the one that goes straight into a drawer and then into the trash. So the test is simple: **the material is natural or reusable, and the item is genuinely useful**.

Here are options that pass both tests.

## 1. Woven bamboo crafts

Bamboo grows fast and can be harvested without replanting from scratch. Woven pieces are also handmade, so each item has its own character.

Practical choices:

- **Bamboo basket bag** as a hamper container. Once the contents are gone, the basket still works for storage.
- **Bamboo food carrier** with a clear lid for food gifts.
- **Bamboo plate** for serving or as a fruit tray.
- **Bamboo tissue box** that goes straight onto a table.

## 2. Bamboo pencil holders

Great for office or school souvenirs. They are small, easy to hand out, and sit on a desk every day. There is a single version and a double version with a handle.

## 3. Coconut shell cups

Coconut shells are usually waste. Turned into cups, they get a new purpose. They look natural and suit rustic or cultural themes.

## 4. Tumblers instead of single-use bottles

One tumbler used daily replaces many plastic bottles. To make sure it gets used, choose one that is comfortable to carry and looks clean.

A tumbler with an **engraved logo** is a safe choice for companies: the logo is etched, not a sticker, so it does not peel off. At Papinka Galeria, Tumbler 001 (500ml) includes engraving in the price.

## 5. Calico tote bags

Tote bags replace plastic shopping bags. Pay attention to the material:

- **Calico (blacu)** is unbleached cotton. This is the most eco-friendly option.
- **Spunbond** is lighter and cheaper, but it is plastic-based (polypropylene). It is still reusable, just not a natural material.
- **Polyester** is strong and lasts a long time.

If your event has an eco theme, choose calico.

## 6. Bottle bags for tumblers

A bottle bag makes a tumbler easier to carry, so it gets used more often. A tumbler and bottle bag together also look more complete as a gift.

## Tips to make sure souvenirs get used

- **Choose function first, then design.** Useful items get used; useless ones do not, however good they look.
- **Keep the logo small and neat.** People are happier to use items that do not look like advertising.
- **Cut plastic packaging.** Wrap with paper, fabric, or a bamboo basket instead.
