A memorable hamper is not only about what is inside. **The container** matters too. Cardboard and plastic get thrown away, while a bamboo container is still in use months after the holiday.

## Choose a useful container

- **Bamboo food carrier** with a clear lid. The contents are visible, ideal for cookies and food.
- **Bamboo basket bag** for hampers with many items. Once empty, it can hold fruit or household items.
- **Bamboo tote basket** with a hexagonal weave for a more distinctive, premium look.
- **Bamboo plate** as the base of an open hamper.

## Hamper filling ideas

- **Cookies or regional specialties.**
- **Coconut shell cups** for a natural touch.
- **Enamel mugs** with the recipient's name or a holiday greeting.
- **Tumblers** with your company logo for client hampers.
- **Bamboo tissue box** as a household item that gets used right away.

## Eid (Lebaran) hampers

Eid is about visiting family and friends, and about cookies. Bamboo containers suit its warm, traditional feel.

- Common colors: green, gold, and cream.
- Add a card with an Eid greeting and the sender's name.
- For clients, choose contents the whole family can enjoy.

## Christmas hampers

Christmas is about sharing and family time.

- Common colors: red, green, and white.
- A colored enamel mug with the recipient's name becomes an everyday keepsake.
- Pair a bamboo basket with a fabric ribbon to keep it eco-friendly.

## Tips for preparing hampers

1. **Set a budget per hamper** before choosing the container and contents.
2. **Match the container size to the contents.** An oversized container makes the hamper look empty.
3. **Use natural fillers** such as shredded paper or dry straw instead of plastic.
4. **Order before the busy season.** Custom orders usually increase close to Eid and Christmas.
5. **Personalize with a card or tag**, not by sticking labels on the container.
