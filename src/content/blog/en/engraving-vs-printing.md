Before ordering custom souvenirs, there is one important question: **which technique will put your logo or design on the product?** The answer depends on the product material and your design.

## Summary

| Technique | Best for | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Engraving | Metal tumblers and mugs | Permanently etched logo, single tone |
| Single-color screen printing | Tote bags and fabric bags | One solid color, clean for simple logos |
| Full-color DTF | Tote bags and fabric bags | Many colors, gradients, and detail |
| Full print | Mugs | Design covers the whole surface |

## Engraving

Engraving etches the product surface so the logo becomes part of the material itself. It does not peel off and looks elegant.

**Choose engraving if:** the product is a metal tumbler or mug, and you want a premium feel for clients or formal gifts.

**Keep in mind:** engraving produces a single tone. Logos with gradients or many colors are converted to one color. Very fine details may not read well.

## Single-color screen printing

Ink is printed onto fabric in one color. Best for simple logos with bold shapes.

**Choose single-color printing if:** your design is simple, such as a one-color logo or an event name.

## Full-color DTF

DTF (direct to film) prints a full-color design onto film, which is then transferred to fabric. Gradients, photos, and detailed illustrations all print well.

**Choose DTF if:** your design is colorful, uses gradients, or is an illustration.

Single-color printing and full-color DTF are available for bottle/tumbler bags and book/document bags.

## Full print

The design is printed across the entire mug surface, not just a small area. Great for illustrations, patterns, or photo collages.

**Choose full print if:** you want a truly personal mug covered in design, like the Full Print Enamel Mug with a knitted handle.

## How to prepare your design file

1. **Use a vector file** (AI, SVG, PDF) if you have one. It can be enlarged without losing quality.
2. **If you do not have a vector, send a high-resolution PNG** with a transparent background.
3. **For engraving, prepare a single-color version** of your logo.
4. **Avoid very thin lines** and very small text.
5. **Ask for a mockup before production** so the logo position and size are exactly what you want.
