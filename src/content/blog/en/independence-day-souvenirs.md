Every August, offices, schools, and neighborhoods in Indonesia celebrate Independence Day (17 August). Souvenirs and competition prizes are almost always needed. To make them more than a formality, choose items that **keep being used after August is over**.

## Competition prizes

Tiered prizes feel fair and keep the budget under control.

- **1st place:** an engraved tumbler. Tumbler 001 (500ml) includes logo or text engraving.
- **2nd place:** a mug with a logo or the words "HUT RI ke-81" (81st Independence Day). The Lidded Custom Logo Mug (350ml) comes in red and white.
- **3rd place and participants:** a tote bag or bookmark. Light, affordable, and still useful.

## Office celebrations

Office events usually include every employee, so matching items are more practical.

- **Mugs or tumblers** with the company logo and an Independence Day message.
- **Book/document bags** printed in red and white, useful for everyday work.

## Neighborhood and community events

For neighborhood associations, youth groups, or communities, quantities are often large and budgets small.

- **Wayang bookmarks** add an Indonesian cultural touch at an affordable price.
- **Colored enamel mugs** as a thank-you for the organizing committee.
- **Tote bags** that can be used for shopping right away.

## Design tips for Independence Day souvenirs

1. **Use red and white in moderation.** A red logo or text on a white product (or the reverse) is strong enough.
2. **Add the theme and year.** For example "HUT RI ke-81" for 2026. Dated souvenirs make better keepsakes.
3. **Keep it simple.** A clean design keeps the item usable outside August.
4. **Order before July.** Orders usually pile up close to August. Order early to keep production on schedule.
