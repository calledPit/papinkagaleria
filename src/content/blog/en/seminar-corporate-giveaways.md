Seminar giveaways are the items handed out to participants at seminars, training sessions, work meetings, product launches, or agency events. The right items keep participants comfortable during the event **and** remind them of the organizer afterward.

## Useful goodie bag items

### Seminar bag

It holds everything else and is the item most often reused. A book/document bag fits folders, books, and a thin laptop. Available in polyester, calico, or spunbond, with single-color or full-color DTF printing.

### Agenda book

Participants need to take notes. An agenda book with your logo on the cover often gets used again at work, so your logo stays visible longer. Ask us about custom agenda books on WhatsApp.

### Bookmark

A low-cost companion to the agenda book. Options include wayang, painting, and wooden designs.

### Pencil holder or stationery

A bamboo pencil holder suits events with an eco or local-culture theme, and after the event it moves to the participant's desk.

## For speakers and VIP guests

Speakers and guests of honor should get something different from participants.

- **Tumbler** with the logo and event name, such as the Bottle Tumbler with Handle Lid (500ml) or an engraved tumbler for a more premium feel.
- **Bamboo craft hamper** filled with local products as a thank-you.

## For government agencies and public events

- **Include the agency logo, event name, and year.** It helps with documentation and makes the item more memorable.
- **Separate participant and speaker packages** from the start so the budget is clear.
- **Mention any administrative needs when ordering**, such as a formal price quotation, so they can be prepared together.

## Example packages

| Package | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| Basic participant | Document bag + bookmark |
| Full participant | Document bag + agenda book + bookmark + pencil holder |
| Speaker / VIP | Logo tumbler + bamboo hamper |

## Tips

1. **Start with the number of participants and the budget per person.** Then choose the contents.
2. **Keep the design consistent across items.** Matching colors and logos make the package look professional.
3. **Order well in advance** and share your event date when ordering.
