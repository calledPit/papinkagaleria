Souvenirs that are cared for properly can be used for years. Here is how to care for each type of product.

## Stainless tumblers

- **Wash with warm water and dish soap** using a soft sponge or bottle brush.
- **Avoid steel wool and bleach.** Both can scratch the outer coating and damage the engraving or color.
- **Dry with the lid off** so it does not stay damp and smell.
- **Do not microwave.** Metal is not safe in a microwave.
- **Hand wash.** Dishwashers can fade the colored outer coating.
- **Coffee or tea smell lingering?** Soak in warm water with a little baking soda, then rinse.

## Enamel mugs

- **Do not drop or knock them.** Enamel can chip on hard impact.
- **Wash with a soft sponge.** Avoid rough scourers to keep the surface and print smooth.
- **For mugs with a knitted handle**, wash the knitted part gently and let it dry completely.

## Tote bags and bottle bags

- **Hand wash in cold water**, especially printed bags.
- **Do not scrub hard** over the print.
- **Iron from the inside of the bag**, never directly on the print.
- **Dry in the shade** so the fabric and print do not fade quickly.

## Bamboo crafts

- **Clean with a damp cloth**, then dry. Do not soak.
- **Store in a dry, well-ventilated place.** Damp bamboo can grow mold.
- **Avoid long exposure to strong sun**, as bamboo can crack.
- **If mold spots appear**, wipe with a cloth dampened with a little vinegar, then air dry completely.

## Coconut shell cups

- **Hand wash** with mild soap, then dry right away.
- **Do not soak for long** and do not put them in a dishwasher or microwave.
- **Occasionally rub with a thin layer of coconut oil** to keep the surface from drying out and keep its shine.

## Summary

| Product | Do | Avoid |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Stainless tumbler | Hand wash, dry with lid off | Bleach, steel wool, microwave |
| Enamel mug | Soft sponge | Hard knocks |
| Printed tote bag | Hand wash in cold water, iron inside out | Scrubbing the print |
| Bamboo crafts | Wipe with a damp cloth, store dry | Soaking, damp storage |
| Coconut shell cup | Hand wash, coconut oil | Long soaking, microwave |
