Wedding favors often end up in a cupboard, never used. A useful favor, on the other hand, reminds guests of your day every time they use it.

Here are practical ideas, plus tips for choosing.

## For your guests

### Enamel mugs with your names and date

Enamel mugs look classic and suit both rustic and modern themes. Add your names and wedding date. Choose the White Enamel Mug (350ml), or the Colored Enamel Mug (350ml) if you want colors that match your theme.

### Mini tumblers

They are just the right size for everyday use, so guests are likely to use them. Options include the Mini Tumbler with Wooden Handle (300ml) and the Pastel Mini Tumbler (400ml). Pastel colors suit softer wedding themes.

### Coconut shell cups

A unique choice for traditional, cultural, or outdoor weddings. The material is natural and every cup has its own texture.

### Bookmarks

Light, easy to hand out, and they take up no space. Great for couples who love reading or for weddings with many guests. Options include wayang, painting, and wooden designs.

## For hampers and wedding gifts

A good container makes the contents look more special, and the container can be used after the event.

- **Bamboo food carrier** with a clear lid, so the contents are visible.
- **Bamboo basket bag** for food or essentials hampers.
- **Bamboo tote basket** for a more distinctive look.

## Tips for choosing wedding favors

1. **Match your theme.** Bamboo and coconut shell for rustic or traditional themes; pastel mugs and tumblers for modern ones.
2. **Personalize lightly.** Small, neat names and a date are more likely to be used than a large photo of the couple.
3. **Order extra.** More guests often attend than expected. Add a few percent.
4. **Order well in advance.** Custom items need production time. Share your wedding date when ordering so the schedule can be confirmed.
5. **Think about how guests take them home.** Light items that do not break easily travel better.
